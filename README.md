## About KeyBack

KeyBack Wallet Recovery is an independent service focused on evaluating recovery possibilities for encrypted Bitcoin and legacy cryptocurrency wallets.

We assist legitimate wallet owners who have lost access to encrypted wallet backups, forgotten passwords, or encountered compatibility issues with older wallet software.

Our approach emphasizes technical feasibility, privacy, transparent communication, and owner authorization.

**We do not guarantee successful recovery.**

## Wallet Recovery Specializations

We assess recovery feasibility for:

### Bitcoin Core
- Encrypted `wallet.dat` files
- Forgotten wallet passwords
- Legacy Bitcoin Core wallet backups

### MultiBit Classic & MultiBit HD
- Legacy encrypted wallet files
- Password-related access problems
- Encrypted `.key` exports
- Backup compatibility issues

### Electrum
- Password-protected wallet files
- Legacy Electrum wallet formats
- Partial-password recovery scenarios

### Blockchain.com / Blockchain.info Legacy
- Encrypted `wallet.aes.json` backups
- Legacy wallet password problems
- Historical encrypted wallet formats

### Ethereum & Other Formats
- Ethereum encrypted keystore JSON files
- Certain legacy MetaMask vault formats
- BIP38-encrypted private keys
- Selected partial-password recovery cases

**Technical feasibility depends on the wallet format, backup integrity, available information, and authorization.**

## Our Recovery Assessment Process

**Step 1 — Initial Inquiry**

The wallet owner describes the wallet software, backup format, access problem, and any previous recovery attempts.

**Step 2 — Free Preliminary Feasibility Assessment**

We review the available non-sensitive technical details to determine whether a realistic recovery path may exist.

**Step 3 — Scope and Terms**

If further work appears feasible, we explain the proposed scope, limitations, and applicable success-based fee before proceeding.

**Step 4 — Owner-Controlled Recovery**

The wallet owner retains control of their cryptocurrency assets. KeyBack does not provide custodial wallet management.

## Security, Privacy & Responsible Recovery

Our operating principles include:

- Wallet-owner authorization is required.
- We do not request private keys or recovery phrases in public discussions.
- Sensitive wallet data must never be posted publicly.
- Original wallet backups should be preserved without modification.
- No guaranteed recovery claims.
- No claims that wallet decryption can reverse confirmed blockchain transactions.
- No custody or management of customer cryptocurrency assets.

**Never publish wallet files, passwords, seed phrases, private keys, or sensitive recovery information in GitHub issues.**
